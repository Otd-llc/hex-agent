Direct replays re-apply only what the configurator accepted in the recorded run, in order, through
`window.hexAPI` (`bin/replay.mjs` is the same mechanism); the camera is moved with the public
`view()` call; no model runs. They do not show the model's messages or tool calls; the engine-room
captions quote the trace's model text verbatim (events `model.text`) at the moment it was sent.
The live segment is the only footage with a model running. Nothing has been printed.

## Music

Generated by the owner with Suno (paid plan, commercial use for songs made and downloaded while
subscribed) from a brief written for this cut: instrumental, 120 BPM so the bars match the grid,
D major, one hook, a single peak at the live run, a clear ending. Cut to the bar grid, the melody
stem ducked under the voice, mastered to about −15 LUFS. Licence line: "Suno, <plan>, generated
2026-11-04, WAV download". No library music. (An in-house bed made in REAPER through the
otd-daw-reaper skill was auditioned and rejected; the skill keeps the record.)

## Furniture

The academy cut's kinetic grammar: hero words key-strike in character by character or snap
together from two halves, with the hollow stroke period; quotes in boxed panels with a label
rule and corner ticks; truth chips (REPLAY, LIVE, RENDER) in the same type; the OTD and Hex
Cluster lockup at the open and the end. Fonts: Bebas Neue, Lora, Space Mono (OFL).
